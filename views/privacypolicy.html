Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve ShortenIt, we collect certain types of information when you interact with our platform:

  • Account Data: Your chosen username and hashed password credentials upon registration.
  • URL Data: The original long URLs you submit and the shortened URLs created under your account.
  • Analytics Data: Non-personally identifiable information regarding link clicks, including Country from IP addresses, Device from user agent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and secure the ShortenIt service.
  • To display click analytics and link performance metrics inside your account dashboard.
  • To detect, prevent, and block malicious activity, phishing attempts, and spam URLs.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential session cookies strictly necessary for keeping you logged into your account safely. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site tracking cookies or sell your browsing history to advertising networks.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

UrlZen does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share anonymized click traffic or disclose information only if required by law enforcement, court order, or to protect the physical safety and security of our platform.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure database storage to safeguard your credentials and link records against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Your Rights

You retain full control over your data. You may delete any shortened links or request the complete deletion of your account and associated history directly through your account settings or by contacting support.
